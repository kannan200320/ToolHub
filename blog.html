Frequently Asked Equipment & Safety Questions

Everything you need to know about operator qualifications, fuel policies, delivery logistics, and safety protocols.

Do I need a special commercial license to rent an excavator or boom lift?

No special commercial license (CDL) is required for homeowners or contractors operating machinery under 10,000 lbs on private jobsite property. However, if you plan to tow the equipment yourself, your personal tow vehicle must meet certified gross trailer weight ratings. ToolHub provides complimentary loading safety checks or full jobsite flatbed delivery.

What is ToolHub's fuel policy upon return?

All gas and diesel machinery is provided with a 100% full fuel tank or fully charged battery packs. Renters can either return the machine full to avoid refueling charges, or opt for our at-cost depot refueling service with zero penalty surcharges.

How does the Zero-Liability Damage Waiver protect me?

Our optional damage waiver relieves renters of financial responsibility for accidental jobsite damage, including blown hydraulic hoses, punctured tires, body scratches, or rock dents up to $50,000. It covers normal field incidents so your security deposit is safely returned.

What weather conditions prevent aerial boom lift operation?

OSHA and ANSI standards mandate grounding aerial boom platforms whenever sustained winds or gusts exceed 28 MPH (12.5 m/s). If carrying large sheet materials that catch wind like a sail, work must cease at 15 MPH. Boom lifts must also never be operated within 10 feet of live electrical power lines.

How fast is the security deposit escrow refunded?

Because deposits are pre-authorization holds rather than settled charges, the hold is electronically released immediately upon completing the 60-second depot return check-in. Funds reappear in your available credit or checking balance within minutes.
