Power Tools • Model DOC-770 PDF Manual (4.2 MB)

Husqvarna K770 14" Handheld Gas Cut-Off Saw

High-torque 2-stroke concrete cutter for cured concrete slabs, asphalt, granite curbs, and rebar. Equipped with Active Air Filtration and SmartCarb system.

Startup & Ignition Sequence

1. Press decompression valve on cylinder head. 2. Pull choke knob up. 3. Pump primer bulb 6 times. 4. Hold base with foot, pull starter cord briskly until cough, push choke in, pull once more to run.

Critical Safety Rules

Always connect water hose for wet cutting to prevent crystalline silica inhalation. Never cut above shoulder height. Ensure blade rotation matches guard arrow.

Includes ANSI Table 1 Guide
Earthmoving • Model DOC-KX40 PDF Manual (8.6 MB)

Kubota KX040-4 Compact Excavator with Hydraulic Thumb

4-ton hydraulic excavator with 11.2ft dig depth, angle blade, and thumb clamp. Standard pattern control changer (ISO to SAE) located beneath seat floorboard.

Pre-Operation Walkaround Check

Check engine oil and hydraulic sight glass with boom rested on flat ground. Inspect rubber tracks for 1-1.5 inch deflection sag. Verify backup beeper and travel alarm.

Trench Safety & Slope Limits

Verify 811 utility marks before ground penetration. Never operate on slopes exceeding 20° (36% grade). Lower blade whenever digging to anchor stability.

Includes Load Lift Chart
Painting • Model DOC-GR695 PDF Manual (3.8 MB)

Graco Ultra Max II 695 Electric Airless Paint Sprayer

Commercial paint and elastomeric coating sprayer capable of driving up to 300ft of fluid hose and two spray guns simultaneously with SmartControl 3.0.

Pressure Relief Procedure

Turn power switch OFF. Engage trigger safety. Turn pressure control to lowest setting. Hold gun against metal pail edge, release trigger lock and pull trigger to relieve pressure. Turn prime valve down.

Skin Injection Hazard Warning

High pressure fluid can penetrate skin and cause severe tissue amputation. Never point spray gun at any body part. Never wipe tip with fingers—use reversible RAC X switch tips.

Includes RAC X Tip Sizing Chart
Ladders • Model DOC-W6228 PDF Manual (2.1 MB)

Werner D6228-2 28ft Type IA Fiberglass Extension Ladder

300lb Duty Rated heavy industrial non-conductive fiberglass extension ladder. Features Shu-Lok dual action foot pads, pulley rope system, and Mar-resistant end caps.

The 4:1 Angle Rule

For every 4 feet of ladder length between ground and upper support, set the base 1 foot out from the wall. Ensure 3 feet of ladder extension above roof eaves.

Rung Lock & 3-Point Contact

Both gravity-lock rung dogs must fully seat over rungs before climbing. Always maintain 3 points of contact (two hands and a foot, or two feet and a hand) while ascending or descending.

OSHA 1926.1053 Compliant
Gardening • Model DOC-MS250 PDF Manual (3.5 MB)

Stihl MS 250 18" Commercial Gas Chainsaw

Mid-sized powerhouse arborist chainsaw with 45.4cc 2-stroke engine and side-access chain tensioner. Equipped with QuickStop inertia-activated chain braking system.

50:1 Fuel Mix & Bar Lubrication

Use Stihl 50:1 2-cycle oil mixed with 89+ octane ethanol-free gasoline. Top up bar oil reservoir at every refueling stop. Verify oil sling onto cardboard before cutting.

Reactive Kickback Zone Caution

Never allow the upper tip (kickback zone) of the guide bar to contact wood or debris. Wrap thumbs completely around handlebars. Always wear chainsaw chaps with Kevlar ballistic fiber.

Includes Chain Sharpening Guide
Power Tools • Model DOC-BH2770 PDF Manual (2.9 MB)

Bosch 60lb Brute Breaker 1-1/8" Hex Electric Jackhammer

35 ft-lbs impact energy demolition hammer with Active Vibration Control (AVC) in hammer mechanism and ergonomic grip handles. Runs on standard 120V 15A circuit.

Power Extension Cord Gauge

Always use minimum 12-gauge heavy extension cords under 50ft (or 10-gauge up to 100ft). Undersized wire causes motor overheating and voided warranty.

Chisel Insertion & Weight Technique

Rotate tool retainer latch 180° to lock bit notch. Allow the 60lb machine mass to do the fracturing—do NOT lean full body weight onto handles or pry sideways.

Includes Moil Point & Chisel Guide
